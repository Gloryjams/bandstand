# Names and mascots

Bandstand, SaltyCharts, Glory Jams, Bones, Bonito and Pinch are trademarks of
Simon Keyser Petty. The AGPL licence covers the code, not the names.

- You may say your software is based on Bandstand.
- You may not call a modified version "Bandstand" or use the mascots to suggest
  that it is official.
- Running an unmodified copy for your own band is always fine.

The mascot artwork has separate permissions in [NOTICE](NOTICE).
